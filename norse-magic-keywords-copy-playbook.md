## 3. BAD / AVOID KEYWORDS

**Trademark / IP (takedown risk):** "Vikings" the TV show (never with Ragnar/Lagertha/Floki) ·
God of War / Kratos · MCU-style Loki · MCU-style Thor (Disney enforces on Etsy) ·
Assassin's Creed / Eivor · Pandora · Tolkien / dwarvish / elvish runes / Cirth.
**Etsy-policy / misleading:** "magic"/"spell"/"cursed" as a *functional* claim (Etsy bans
selling actual spells — describe the object, not a guaranteed effect) · "authentic Viking" /
"ancient" on modern-made items · "genuine [material]" unless verifiable.
**Wasted single-word tags:** gift · jewelry · necklace · handmade · pagan (alone). Always modify.
**Far-right-contaminated (brand/algorithm risk — awareness):**
- NEVER USE: Black Sun / Sonnenrad · Wolfsangel · "88" · kek/Pepe · "blood and soil".
- AVOID AS POSITIONING: "folkish" (signals ethnonationalist exclusion — repels your best buyers).
- HANDLE WITH CARE: Othala / Tyr rune (legit, but avoid SS-adjacent styling; legged-Othala = SS rune).
*(Confidence HIGH on trademark + hate-symbol items; MED on borderline policy terms.)*

## 5. ETSY TITLE/TAG MECHANICS (2025–26)

**Title:** 140 chars but only ~55–60 show before truncation → FRONT-LOAD primary phrase.
≤15 words. Pipe-separated. Formula = *product type + material/method + cultural modifier +
use/occasion*. Sept-2025 algo rewards readable titles, penalizes stuffing.
Example: `Elder Futhark Rune Set | Handmade Wooden Viking Runes with Bag | Norse Pagan Divination Gift`

**Tags:** use ALL 13; each a multi-word phrase (≤20 chars); don't duplicate title phrases;
cover adjacent variants; exact-match beats scattered words. Cover 13 dimensions: core product,
material, symbol, cultural modifier, practitioner identity, use case, gifting occasion,
aesthetic, specificity variant, broad crossover, material+occasion, long-tail conversion,
alternate cultural term.

**Ready tag block (wooden Elder Futhark set w/ bag):**
`elder futhark runes` · `wooden rune set` · `rune casting set` · `viking rune set` ·
`norse pagan gift` · `divination rune set` · `handmade rune stones` · `rune casting bag` ·
`asatru gift` · `pagan divination` · `heathen gift` · `rune set with bag` · `personalized runes`

**Attributes:** fill every one (2025 added ranking weight); Occasion=Spiritual/Wedding/Birthday
feeds gifting pages. **Description:** first 160 chars are Google-indexed — keyword there too.

## 6. MATCH-THEN-BEAT: THE COMPETITION

**Who wins:** crystal-generalist shops on review-count social proof + guidebook bundles —
TheTarotStore (~16.8k reviews/listing), TheStoneSanctuary (62k+ shop-wide), MOONLIGHTSTUDIOUS
(~$10–12, ~13k reviews). Dedicated Norse/custom shops (NorseTradesman, Nornir, 3rdHouseDesigns)
= smaller, higher AOV. *(Review counts as sales proxy; Etsy hides sales.)*

**TABLE-STAKES CHECKLIST (must-match):**
- [ ] 8–10 photos (earthy wood/linen/candlelit backgrounds — NOT stark white)
- [ ] 5–15s in-hand video
- [ ] Title front-loads keyword + material + use case
- [ ] Free shipping folded into price
- [ ] "What's in the box" explicit (rune count, pouch, guide, mat)
- [ ] Personalization option (even "add initials")
- [ ] Reply <24h (aim <2h) → Star Seller
- [ ] Stated handling time + tracked shipping
- [ ] 15–20 listings at launch; consistent aesthetic
- [ ] About page w/ maker photo + origin story
- [ ] ≥1 bundle/gift-set with guidebook

**THEIR WEAKNESSES = YOUR OPENINGS:**
1. Dropshipped AliExpress goods sold as "handmade" → show hands/workshop/process video.
2. Historically inaccurate / fantasy-contaminated (Elder vs Younger Futhark; God-of-War symbols)
   → **source every symbol.**
3. Generic 21-page booklet → sourced lore card / QR "lore library."
4. Serve everyone → generic. Pick practitioner OR aesthetic explicitly.
5. Silent on co-option → explicit inclusive stance wins a loyal, vocal segment.
6. Slow overseas shipping (VKNG/Ukraine, China) → domestic + fast.

## 7. THE UNIQUE TWIST (ranked by defensibility)

1. **★ "The Archaeologist's Rune Set" — a sourced history card in EVERY order.**
   Printed card cites the actual artifact/manuscript ("the Isa rune follows the Codex Runicus,
   c.1300; never historically a hate symbol"). MOST DEFENSIBLE — can't be faked by a dropshipper,
   turns the niche's #1 anxiety (accuracy) into the brand, doubles as free TikTok/IG content,
   scales as a template. This is the signature mechanic.
2. **Inclusive / anti-folkish brand voice** (bio, day one) — free; signals safety to the segment
   that avoids ambiguous shops.
3. **"Made for rune work, not rune display"** — practitioner-grade; loyal, high-LTV, networked
   word-of-mouth.
4. **Custom bind-rune commissions with consultation** — premium AOV, expertise-gated.
5. **"The Solitary's First Year" starter ecosystem** — bundle (set + cloth + small deity piece +
   candle + sourced 40-pg guide); nobody owns this positioning; guide drives email capture.
6. **"The Rune Letter" newsletter** — one sourced rune/month; ~24 issues = a reference + your
   escape from Etsy-algorithm dependency. HIGHEST long-term moat.

**Synthesis:** sourced history card (mechanic) + inclusive voice (safety) + practitioner framing
(loyalty) + the Rune Letter (owned audience). Each plugs a documented competitor weakness; together
they're very hard to replicate.

## 8. COPY DOCTRINE (reusable)
```
- Titles/tags = outsider words (viking, rune, elder futhark). Descriptions/bio = emic (blót, wyrd, the lore).
- Front-load the primary phrase; all 13 multi-word tags; fill attributes.

- Differentiate on SOURCED ACCURACY + INCLUSIVE SAFETY + PRACTITIONER GRADE. Cite the Edda/find in-listing.
- Signature mechanic: a sourced history card in every order.
```
