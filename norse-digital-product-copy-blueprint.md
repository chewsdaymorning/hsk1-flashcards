## 6. HOW THE DESCRIPTION SHOULD LOOK (structure)
Digital buyers need two things resolved fast: **"what exactly do I get?"** and **"how does
delivery/printing work?"** Structure top-to-bottom:

1. **Hook (first 160 chars — Google-indexed):** the benefit + main keyword, naturally.
2. **What you get (bulleted, specific):** page count, every component, formats, sizes, DPI.
3. **What makes it different (the moat):** sourced from the rune poems; honesty pages.
4. **Who it's for:** beginners + practitioners; solitary-friendly.
5. **How it works (digital mechanics):** instant download, no physical item, how to access.
6. **Print specs:** US Letter + A4, 300 DPI, print at home or a print shop.
7. **Terms of use:** personal use; no resale/redistribution of files.
8. **Inclusive line:** all backgrounds welcome; reject racist misuse.
9. **Closing CTA + keyword-rich sign-off.**

**Formatting rules:** short paragraphs, bullets, ALL-CAPS mini-headers (Etsy strips rich text),
emoji sparingly as visual anchors (✦ ⚡ ᚠ), weave emic vocabulary (*the lore, the futhark,
blót, wyrd*) into the body for believability — but keep the title/tags outsider-friendly.

## 8. WHAT MAKES "GOOD" DIGITAL COPY (principles)
- **Specificity sells the intangible:** exact page count, formats, sizes, DPI. Vagueness kills digital conversion.
- **Resolve delivery anxiety early:** "instant download, no physical item, here's where to find it."
- **The differentiator must be a *claim you can prove*** — "sourced from the rune poems" + a sources page. That's the believability lever; competitors citing Blum can't match it.
- **Benefit before features in the hook;** features (the file list) right after.
- **Scannable:** headers, bullets, white space — buyers skim.
- **Set print expectations** (Letter + A4, 300 DPI) to prevent the #1 digital complaint (bad prints).
- **State the license** to preempt resale and refund issues (digital = no returns; say so kindly).
- **Don't overclaim magic** — describe the guide/journal, never a guaranteed supernatural result (Etsy policy + trust).
