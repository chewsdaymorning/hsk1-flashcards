# PART 1 — THE MYTHOLOGY, EXPLAINED (so copy is accurate)

## 1.1 Governing truth (the brand's whole edge)
Nearly all written Norse myth survives in **13th-c. Christian-era Icelandic texts**
(Poetic Edda c.1270; Snorri's Prose Edda/Heimskringla c.1220–30) — 200+ years after
conversion — plus mute archaeology. So: **separate what's attested from what's modern
invention, and say which is which.** That single habit is the differentiator.

## 1.2 The gods & entities (what they governed → use in product themes)
| Entity | Domain (sells as…) | Realm | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Óðinn / Odin** | runes, wisdom, war, frenzy, the dead, poetry | Ásgarðr / Valhöll | "Allfather"; won the runes by self-sacrifice |
| **Freyja** | love, fertility, war-dead, **seiðr (taught it to the gods)** | Fólkvangr | the archetypal völva; necklace Brísingamen |
| **Freyr** | fertility, peace, prosperity, virility | Álfheimr | the *Skírnismál* suitor |
| **Frigg** | marriage, motherhood, knows fate (stays silent) | Fensalir | name = "beloved" |
| **Þórr / Thor** | **protection, hallowing** | Þrúðheimr | the ONLY god invoked on runestones ("Þórr vígi") |
| **Týr** | victory, oaths, law | Ásgarðr | victory-runes (*Sigrdrífumál*) |
| **Hel** | the dead, underworld | Helheim | Loki's daughter |
| **Njörðr** | sea, wind, wealth | Nóatún | father of Freyr & Freyja |
| **The Norns** (Urðr/Verðandi/Skuld) | **fate** | Well of Urðr | carve/allot fate (valkyries do the *weaving*) |
| **Dísir / Álfar / Landvættir** | ancestors / elves / land-spirits | — | honored via blót for protection & fertility |
| **Þursar / Jötnar** | the **feared/hostile** force | Jötunheimr | illness personified as a *þurs* in healing charms |

## 1.3 The kinds of magic (product/content themes)
- **Seiðr** — trance prophecy + fate-shaping (völva on a high-seat, staff, *varðlokur* chants).
- **Galdr** — sung/chanted spells (Hávamál's 18 charms, listed by effect).
- **Runes** — primarily an alphabet; a minority of ritual use (charm-words *alu/laukaz*).
- **Blót** — sacrifice as **reciprocity** ("a gift ever looks for a return," Hávamál 145).
- **Bind-runes / staves, curse-poles (níðstöng), love-compulsion** (Skírnismál).

## 1.4 "Why it worked" (the worldview, for deep copy)
Fate is a woven substance you can read/tug · the self is partly detachable (*hugr/hamr*) so
it can act at a distance · the cosmos is a society of spirits you feed and bargain with ·
words/runes carry real causal force; power is **priced** through sacrifice.

# PART 2 — REAL-WORLD CORRELATIONS (the credibility layer + content hooks)

These connect myth to verifiable reality — gold for "did you know" content and history cards.

- **Weekdays are Norse gods:** Tuesday=Týr (Tiw), Wednesday=Óðinn (Woden), Thursday=Þórr
  (Thunor), Friday=Frigg/Freyja. *Living proof the pantheon is embedded in English.*
- **"Weird" ← *wyrd*** (fate); **"berserk" ← berserkr**; **Bluetooth logo = bind-rune** of
  Haraldr Blátǫnn (H+B). *Everyday language/tech carries Norse DNA.*
- **Real archaeology behind the myth:** Oseberg & Gokstad ships; ~40 völva staff-graves
  (Fyrkat with henbane seeds); Birka Bj 581 (DNA-confirmed woman in a weapon grave);
  ~1,000 Thor's-hammer amulets; the Købelev hammer inscribed "this is a hammer."
- **The rune poems are real, dated texts** (Old English c.9th c.; Norwegian 13th; Icelandic
  15th) — the legitimate source of rune meanings.
- **Honest "NOT Viking" facts** (these *are* the content that builds trust):
  - **Vegvísir** = first attested 1860 (Huld Manuscript). **Helm of Awe graphic** = 17th-c.
    Galdrabók (the *word* is older). **"Web of Wyrd" grid** = 1990s. **Horned helmets** =
    Bronze-Age/1876 Wagner opera (only the Gjermundbu cap is a real Viking helmet).
  - **Ralph Blum's rune meanings** (1982) + the "blank rune" = modern invention, adapted
    from the I Ching.
- **Living modern religion:** Ásatrú is Iceland's largest non-Christian faith, building its
  first temple in ~1,000 years. *(Context that the tradition is alive, not cosplay.)*
- **The dark correlation to manage:** far-right groups co-opt Norse symbols (Othala, Valknut,
  Sonnenrad). Inclusive Heathens reject this (Declaration 127). Your brand states this openly.

## 3.2 LONG-TAIL (where a NEW shop actually wins — low competition, high conversion)
elder futhark rune set wooden · personalized bind rune necklace · protection bind rune pendant ·
vegvisir pendant sterling silver · viking blot bowl hand carved · asatru altar cloth runes ·

**(digital)** elder futhark rune meanings printable · rune poem reference printable ·
historically accurate rune guide · norse pagan book of shadows pages · digital norse grimoire download

## 3.3 BAD / AVOID
- **Trademarks:** Vikings (TV), God of War/Kratos, Assassin's Creed/Eivor, MCU Loki/Thor, Pandora, Tolkien/Cirth.
- **Misleading/policy:** "ancient/authentic Viking" on modern items; "spell"/"guaranteed" as a function claim (Etsy bans selling spells).
- **Wasted single words:** gift, jewelry, necklace, printable, pdf, pagan (alone).
- **Hate-adjacent (brand + algo risk):** "folkish" as positioning, Black Sun, Wolfsangel, "88", kek; SS-styled Othala/Tyr.

## 3.4 THE SEO WE CAN WIN (winnable gaps, ranked)
The mass-market head terms (crystal rune sets, Vegvísir jewelry, POD tees) are **red oceans**
dominated by 16k–62k-review shops — do NOT fight there on the head term. Win by owning:
1. **"Historically accurate / sourced" long-tail** — almost no competitor targets `rune poem
   reference printable`, `historically accurate rune guide`. The accuracy USP *is* a keyword cluster.
2. **Practitioner terms** — `blot bowl`, `asatru altar cloth`, `seidr`, `heathen gift`:
   low listing depth, passionate buyers, thin supply.
3. **Underrepped deities** — Skaði, Hel, Sif, Iðunn (Freyja is crowded).
4. **Norse-specific DIGITAL** — lore-accurate printables vs. generic Wicca = a real gap.
5. **Anglo-Saxon / Old English Rune Poem** angle — blue ocean, same runes reframed.
6. **Inclusive-signal terms** — "pagans against hate," "runes not racism": sparse + loyal.

## 3.5 ETSY MECHANICS (2025–26) — to actually rank
- Title: front-load primary phrase (only ~55–60 chars show), ≤15 words, pipe-separated,
  formula = *product + material/method + cultural modifier + use/occasion*. Readable > stuffed.
- Tags: all **13**, every one **multi-word** (≤20 chars), no title duplicates, exact-match wins.
- Fill **all attributes** (added ranking weight; Occasion=Spiritual feeds gifting pages).
- First **160 chars** of description = Google-indexed → keyword there too.

# PART 5 — HOW TO BE AS GOOD, THEN BEAT THEM

## 5.1 TABLE-STAKES (match before you can beat)
- 8–10 photos on **earthy/candlelit** backgrounds (not stark white) + a 5–15s in-hand/flip video
- Keyword-front-loaded title; free shipping folded into price (physical)
- Explicit "what's in it" (rune count / page count, formats)
- A personalization option; reply <2h → **Star Seller**
- 15–20 listings at launch; consistent aesthetic; About page w/ maker photo; ≥1 bundle w/ guide

## 5.2 THEIR WEAKNESSES = YOUR OPENINGS
1. Dropshipped AliExpress sold as "handmade" → **show hands/workshop/process**.
2. Historically inaccurate / fantasy-contaminated (Elder vs Younger Futhark, God-of-War symbols)
   → **source every symbol** (cite the Edda/find/poem).
3. Generic 21-page booklet → **sourced lore card / "lore library."**
4. Serve-everyone generic → **pick practitioner-grade** explicitly.
5. Silent on co-option → **explicit inclusive stance** wins a loyal, vocal segment.
6. Slow overseas shipping → **domestic + fast** (physical); instant + clear print specs (digital).

## 5.3 THE UNIQUE TWIST (ranked by defensibility)
1. **★ "Sourced history card / page in every product"** — cite the actual artifact/manuscript/
   rune-poem stanza. Undropshippable, turns the niche's #1 anxiety (accuracy) into the brand,
   doubles as free TikTok/IG content. **The signature mechanic.**
2. **Inclusive / anti-folkish brand voice** (bio, day one) — free; signals safety.
3. **"Made for the work, not the shelf"** — practitioner-grade; high-LTV, networked word-of-mouth.
4. **Custom bind-rune commissions w/ consultation** — premium AOV, expertise-gated.
5. **"The Solitary's First Year" bundle** — serves the 64% who practice alone; guide = email capture.
6. **"The Rune Letter" newsletter** — one sourced rune/month; ~24 issues = a reference + your
   escape from Etsy-algorithm dependency. Highest long-term moat.

**Synthesis:** sourced accuracy (mechanic) + inclusive safety + practitioner framing + owned
audience. Each plugs a documented competitor weakness; together they're hard to copy.
