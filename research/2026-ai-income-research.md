## Section 1 — Definitions, Labor Thresholds, Net/Gross Assumptions

**Passive / low-maintenance (bucket A).** Upfront build: 40–150 hours over 1–3 months. Ongoing labor ceiling: **≤4 hrs/week**, trending toward ≤2 after month 6. Pass test: *if he did zero work for 6 months, it still pays* — meaning revenue comes from a catalog, subscription base, or automated pipeline, not from him showing up. Content churn does not qualify unless the catalog compounds or fulfillment is fully automated.

**High-ticket / high-margin (bucket B).** Ongoing labor ceiling: **≤15 hrs/week** at the €5k-net level (this is an *additional* stream, not a job replacement). Pass test: *a small number of clients (≤4) clears €5k net without location-tethering or a 50-hr week.* Anything requiring on-site presence, phone-ops loops, or daily manual delivery is cut or downgraded.

**Net vs. gross assumption (stated once, applied per idea).** He is currently Kleinunternehmer (§19 UStG: ≤€25k prior-year revenue, ≤€100k current-year, 2025 rules). Any idea hitting €5k net/month **blows through the Kleinunternehmer threshold in year one** — so all €5k-net paths assume Regelbesteuerung (standard VAT registration, 19% USt on top of B2B invoices — pass-through for B2B clients, a real 19% price wedge for B2C digital products sold to EU consumers). Effective personal burden at this income level (Einkommensteuer + Soli + voluntary GKV/PV as self-employed) ≈ **35–42%**. Therefore:

- **Services (low COGS):** €5,000 net requires ≈ **€8,300–9,500 gross revenue/month**.
- **Digital products B2C (marketplace fees 10–20% + VAT owed on consumer sales + API costs):** €5,000 net requires ≈ **€10,000–11,500 gross/month**.
- **SaaS/subscriptions (infra + API COGS 15–30%):** ≈ **€9,500–11,000 gross MRR**.

**Named traps (filtered out before scoring).** Evidence-backed exclusions:
- **Horizontal AI-wrapper SaaS** (writing tools, chatbots): ~3% two-year survival, 60–70% never revenue, 43% median annual churn vs 23% traditional SaaS (McKinsey via buildmvpfast.com; ChartMogul 2025). Cut.
- **Generic "AI automation agency"**: guru-saturated; sub-$10k projects and sub-$200/hr rates collapsing; clients openly demanding "AI discounts" (anderscpa.com/McKinsey 2025: ad spend +8.6%, agency revenue −1.2%). Only differentiated/regulated variants scored.
- **Prompt marketplaces / GPT Store**: 3M+ GPTs, no meaningful solo payout mechanism proven. Cut.
- **AI music catalogs**: majors' settlements (Udio/Warner, Nov 2025) + Deezer tagging 13.4M AI tracks + 3-tier royalty demotion on Spotify/Apple. Structural platform risk. Cut.
- **Mass KDP/AI-book publishing**: mandatory AI disclosure, automated velocity detection, no verified aggregate revenue found. Cut.
- **Thin programmatic SEO**: template-injection sites deindexed within days of the May 2026 core update; AI Overviews CTR drops 34–61% (Ahrefs, Seer). Only rich-proprietary-data variants survive — folded into idea #8.
- **Voice-agent reselling**: real revenue exists ($1M ARR case, Ringlyn), but German outbound calling (UWG §7 consent rules) + a live phone-ops loop fails the location/labor tests. Cut with regret.
- **"Sell an AI course"**: excluded by brief; no differentiation argument survives his positioning anyway.

## Section 3 — Top 3 Deep-Dives

### 🥇 #1 — "AI-Act-Ready" Automation Audit → Implementation → Retainer (Mittelstand)

**Why it wins.** It's the intersection of every asset he has and a legally mandated demand spike he didn't have to create: the AI Act's transparency-and-penalties regime goes live **August 2, 2026 — three weeks from now**. Verified pricing says the Mittelstand pays €960–2,500/day for KI-Beratung and €900–3,500 for AI-Act compliance bundles, and BAFA subsidizes the audit so the client's out-of-pocket is €700–1,750 for a €3,500 engagement — the subsidy is the sales pitch. Crucially, generic AI agencies are collapsing on price while the *regulated-and-differentiated* tier (compliance + real agent implementation) is the one segment with verified rate integrity. Two audits plus one implementation per month ≈ €9–13k gross ≈ €5.5–8k net at ~40% burden, inside ~12 hrs/week because his Claude Code pipelines do the heavy lifting on discovery, documentation, and the compliance register. It compounds: every audit feeds implementation work, and every implementation feeds retainer income (idea #2) and productizable skills (idea #3).

**The honest risk.** Sales cycle and trust. Mittelstand buyers are relationship buyers; a solo consultant without references faces a 1–3 month trust gap, and some will expect on-site presence (mitigate: productize as remote workshops + fixed deliverables; do the first two audits hybrid if needed and treat that as launch cost, not the model). Second risk: the compliance angle commoditizes by 2027 as templates flood in — the durable core is the *implementation* skill, so compliance should be the wedge, never the whole offer. Inference, clearly labeled: I judge the post-deadline demand tail to be 12–24 months of elevated urgency, not permanent.

**First-week action.** Register as a BAFA-listed Berater (or verify eligibility) and build one fixed-scope offer page in German: *"KI-Readiness- & AI-Act-Audit — €3.500 Festpreis, bis zu 80 % BAFA-gefördert, Ergebnis: Automatisierungs-Roadmap + AI-Act-Transparenz-Dossier in 10 Tagen."* Then send 20 personalized outreach messages to Wiesbaden/Rhein-Main Mittelstand firms (IHK directory) anchored on the August 2 deadline. One booked audit = validation.

### 🥈 #2 — Claude Skills Catalog + White-Label Agent Pipelines (ideas #3 + #4 as one passive engine)

**Why it wins.** This is the passive-bucket play with the best evidence-to-effort ratio for *him specifically*. Paid skill marketplaces went live late 2025; verified distribution shows top skills earning $500–3k/month recurring at 70–80% creator rev-share, skills portable across 20+ agent harnesses, and "claude agent sdk" search demand up ~295x YoY — an early, land-grab-phase market where his SKILL.md expertise is genuinely rare. Alone, marketplace income is Marginal (median skill <$50/mo — the honest number). The fix is the white-label layer: the same pipelines licensed directly to 10–12 marketing agencies at €400–900/month (white-label AI resell margins verified at 65–80%) turns a long-tail catalog into predictable MRR. Combined path: €5–9k gross MRR ≈ €3–5.5k net at ≤6 hrs/week maintenance — and it passes the 6-month test because skills and licensed pipelines keep billing without him. It's also the highest Fun score on the board: it *is* his craft.

**The honest risk.** Platform risk squared: the marketplaces are young (could pivot, cut rev-share, or die), and Anthropic's own Managed Agents releases keep absorbing adjacent functionality — a skill that's a thin wrapper gets Sherlocked. Mitigation is the ChatGPT-test: only ship skills embedding real workflow IP (his approval-gate orchestration, character-consistency pipelines, compliance-document generators from idea #1), and keep the agency-direct licensing channel as the platform-independent majority of revenue.

**First-week action.** Package his two most battle-tested internal pipelines as sellable skills, list both on Agensi and Agent 37 (48 hours of packaging, he already has the assets), and simultaneously pitch three agencies he can reach warm with a white-label demo of the UGC-ad pipeline. Signal to read: any organic marketplace sale or one agency LOI within 14 days.

### 🥉 #3 — Etsy Pipeline Cloning + Multi-Marketplace Catalog Compounding

**Why it wins.** He already owns the hardest part: a *proven, running, end-to-end automated fulfillment pipeline* — the thing 95% of "AI digital product" aspirants never build. The 2025–2026 evidence says the surviving Etsy segment is exactly his: personalized, disclosure-compliant, human-designed-AI-fulfilled products (generic template shops are the ones dying under the June 2025 Creativity Standards). The move is horizontal cloning, not more work on one shop: replicate the pipeline into 2–3 adjacent personalization niches (the hermetic-readings engine generalizes to any structured-personalization vertical), and mirror the catalog on Gumroad (AI-utility product transactions +150% YoY, $206M platform revenue) plus an own-domain Shopify to cap platform risk. Each additional shop is mostly configuration, not construction — that's catalog compounding, the only way a content-adjacent business passes the passive test. Realistic: existing shop + 2 clones reaching €8–11k gross/month combined within 6–9 months ≈ €4–5.5k net (B2C burden is heavier: fees + OSS VAT).

**The honest risk.** Platform enforcement is the big one — Etsy's automated system reportedly mis-flags ~38% of borderline listings, and a suspension of the flagship shop overnight is a real scenario (hence the multi-platform mirror is non-negotiable, not optional). Saturation risk is the highest of the top 3 (scored 4): personalization and Schwartz-grade copy are the differentiators, and if a clone niche doesn't show sales velocity in 30 days, kill it and pick the next — the pipeline makes niche tests cheap.

**First-week action.** Pick one adjacent niche by mining his existing shop's customer messages and Etsy search data for unmet personalization demand, then stand up the cloned pipeline with 10 listings AND mirror the current bestsellers onto Gumroad. Both are executable inside a week with the existing infrastructure; first cross-platform sale = validation.

## Section 4 — Verdict

**Start the AI-Act-Ready Mittelstand audit (idea #1) — this week, not this quarter.** It is the only idea on the board with a hard external clock working in his favor: the August 2, 2026 enforcement date creates urgency he doesn't have to manufacture, the BAFA subsidy (expiring Dec 31, 2026) nearly pre-sells the audit, and it monetizes his rarest asset — production-grade agent architecture — at verified German market rates where two clients a month clear the €5k-net floor. Just as important, it's the trunk of the tree: every audit generates the compliance templates and pipeline IP that feed the passive engines (#2's skill catalog and white-label licenses, and even #3's operational tooling), so the high-ticket stream funds and de-risks the passive streams instead of competing with them. Run #1 as the income floor now, layer #2 in month two as the passivity play, and treat #3 as the already-running asset to compound in the background.
